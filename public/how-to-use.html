How to Use WeatherFlow

Master your daily atmospheric intelligence with live MET Norway forecasts, genuine air quality data, rain planners, and Android 14 home screen widgets.

1 First Time Setup & Location Access

When launching WeatherFlow for the first time, you can choose either automatic GPS detection or manually searching for your favorite cities:

  • Automatic GPS: Grant "While using the app" location access. WeatherFlow uses your latitude and longitude, automatically rounding them to 4 decimal places (~11 meters) for total privacy.
  • Manual City Search: If you prefer not to share GPS coordinates, tap the Places tab on the bottom bar, type any global city (e.g. Surat, Oslo, Tokyo, New York), and tap Save.
💡 Tip: Tap the city dropdown directly at the top of the Today screen to switch between saved locations in one tap.

2 Understanding the Today Screen

The Today screen is your meteorological command center:

🌡️ Temperature & Scale Toggle

Tap °C ⇄ °F at the top bar to instantly convert between Celsius and Fahrenheit globally across all cards and widgets.

⏱️ Freshness & Model Run Pill

Check the top pill for cache age and MET Norway update time. Tap the info icon to jump directly into the Forecast Trust Center.

🧭 Barometer 3-Hour Trend

See live hPa pressure and atmospheric delta. Rapid drops indicate incoming precipitation or thunderstorms.

☀️ Sun & Moon Trajectory

Tracks astronomical sunrise, solar noon, sunset, and live lunar phase (waxing/waning, % illuminated).

3 Reading Real-Time Air Quality & Pollutants

Unlike apps that estimate air quality using fake synthetic formulas, WeatherFlow integrates genuine CAMS and NOAA atmospheric chemical readings:

  • US AQI Index: Scaled 0 to 500 in accordance with EPA standards (0–50 Good, 51–100 Moderate, 101–150 Sensitive, 151–200 Unhealthy, 201–300 Very Unhealthy, 301+ Hazardous).
  • Individual Chemical Readouts: Check real microgram concentrations for Fine Particles (PM2.5), Coarse Dust (PM10), Ground Ozone (O₃), and Nitrogen Dioxide (NO₂).
  • Outdoor Cardio Safety: Evaluates running, cycling, and walking safety scores out of 100 based on pollution and heat index.

4 Rain Planner & Convective Uncertainty

Microbursts and convective precipitation cells fluctuate across kilometers. WeatherFlow provides honest, scientific probability windows rather than deceptive exact-minute countdowns:

  • Window Clusters: Identifies upcoming precipitation clusters (e.g. 14:00 – 17:00, 1.2 mm accumulation).
  • Peak Rate & Confidence: Shows maximum millimeters per hour and confidence levels based on atmospheric moisture divergence.

5 Adding Home Screen Widgets

WeatherFlow includes 5 dynamic Material You widget form factors with frosted glass and OLED black modes:

  1. Long press any empty area on your phone's home screen.
  2. Tap Widgets and scroll down to find WeatherFlow.
  3. Select from:
    • 1x1 Quick Glance: Temperature badge and condition icon.
    • 4x1 Modern Pill: Sleek horizontal pill with high/low and precipitation.
    • 2x2 Radial Dial: Circular thermal indicator with wind speed and humidity.
    • 4x2 Full Forecast Matrix: Multi-day forecast with hourly projection.
    • 3x3 Atmospheric Radar: Radar preview, air quality gauge, and sun tracker.
  4. Drag and drop the widget onto your home screen!
✨ Widgets Studio: Visit the You tab inside the app > Home & Lockscreen Widgets Studio to preview widgets, adjust glass transparency sliders, and toggle OLED black mode before adding them.

6 Activity Planner & Personal Comfort Sliders

Under the Plan and You tabs, customize your personal comfort envelope:

  • Set your Minimum Ideal Temp (e.g. 16°C) and Maximum Ideal Temp (e.g. 24°C).
  • WeatherFlow dynamically calculates suitability scores (0–100%) for Running, Cycling, Walking, and Outdoor Workouts based on your thermal tolerance, wind gust limits, and air quality thresholds.
