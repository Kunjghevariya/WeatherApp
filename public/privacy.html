1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

O Square Apps ("we", "us", or "our") builds mobile experiences founded on user trust, privacy, and scientific transparency. This Privacy Policy governs your use of WeatherFlow: Live Weather & AQI (the "Application" or "App").

We believe meteorological intelligence should never cost your personal privacy. We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal identifiers (such as your name, email, phone number, or contacts) to use the App.

2. Location Data & Coordinate Precision Safeguards

To deliver real-time atmospheric readings, precipitation alerts, and local solar/lunar ephemeris, WeatherFlow requires access to your device's geographical position:

  • Foreground Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Used when the app is active to automatically identify your local weather station, air quality index, and rain planner.
  • Background Updates (Optional for Widgets & Alerts): Used periodically via Android WorkManager solely to refresh active home screen widgets and dispatch timely severe storm warnings.

🔒 4-Decimal Coordinate Truncation (~11m Precision): Before any geographic coordinate leaves your device to query atmospheric APIs, our engine strictly truncates latitude and longitude to exactly four decimal places. This ensures high meteorological accuracy without ever exposing your exact residential or street address.

We never record, log, or sell your location history. Location coordinates are strictly processed in-memory and cached locally on your device.

3. Meteorological & Third-Party Services

WeatherFlow accesses public meteorological and telemetry providers to provide live weather predictions:

  • MET Norway (Meteorologisk Institutt): Primary atmospheric and precipitation forecasts are fetched from MET Norway's Locationforecast 2.0 API under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). Requests are transmitted over encrypted TLS.
  • Open-Meteo Air Quality & CAMS: Atmospheric pollutant measurements (PM2.5, PM10, O₃, NO₂, CO) are fetched from Open-Meteo leveraging the European Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS) and NOAA models.
  • Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC): We collect aggregated, non-personalized operational metrics (such as crash telemetry, app cold start latency, and feature usage) to detect bugs and optimize performance. No advertising ID (AAID) is used to link your identity.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): To keep WeatherFlow accessible, non-intrusive banner or interstitial advertisements may be served in accordance with Google Play policies and IAB Tech Lab app-ads.txt specifications (Publisher ID: pub-7787849898368360).

4. Local Storage & Data Retention

All user-configured preferences—including saved cities, thermal comfort limits, active temperature scales (°C/°F), and widget appearance configurations—are stored locally on your device in an encrypted Room SQLite database and DataStore preferences.

You may purge all cached forecasts and saved places at any time by selecting "Clear Data / Storage" in your device's Android Application Settings.

5. Google Play Data Safety Disclosures

  • Location: Approximate and Precise Location collected for App Functionality, Personalization, and Widget Updates. Encrypted in transit. Not shared with third-party data brokers.
  • App Info and Performance: Crash logs and diagnostic metrics collected via Firebase for stability analysis.
  • Device or other IDs: Ephemeral Firebase installation tokens used strictly for analytics and crash prevention.
  • Data Sale: We never sell your personal or device data.

6. Children's Privacy

WeatherFlow complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
